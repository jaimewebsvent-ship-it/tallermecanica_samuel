## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a balanced identity: the grounded reliability of a neighborhood auto repair shop with over 30 years of history, combined with the precision and cleanliness of modern vehicle diagnostics.

### Audience & Mood
- **Target Audience:** Local vehicle owners, families, and commercial fleet drivers in the Actur neighborhood and greater Zaragoza seeking immediate mechanical resolution, routine maintenance, and high-standard bodywork (chapa y pintura).
- **Emotional Response:** Immediate trust, transparency, operational authority, and zero friction for emergency contact or quote estimation.
- **Design Movement:** Clean Industrial Modernism with tactile contrast. It couples clean mechanical whites and cool pearl greys with automotive-grade charcoal anchors and vibrant safety calder/amber-red CTAs. Layouts prioritize clear technical communication, honest quotes, and direct physical-world utility (one-click calling, map access).

## Colors

The color system anchors the service in authoritative deep carbon foundations while maintaining high legibility through crisp pearl backgrounds and urgent, automotive-grade accent tones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#EA580C` - Caldera Orange):** Represents technical vitality, quick service, and primary action items (e.g., "Pedir Cita Previa", primary CTAs, active states).
- **Secondary (`#DC2626` - Vibrant Mechanical Red):** Reserved for urgent actions, critical indicators, phone assistance, and high-visibility status badges (e.g., "Llamar Ahora - Urgencias").
- **Tertiary (`#1F2937` - Industrial Charcoal):** Structural framing color used for footers, contrasting feature strips, hero sections, badges, and deep container layers.
- **Neutral Core (`#111827` - Deep Carbon Black):** High-contrast body typography, strong headline titles, and high-emphasis boundaries.
- **Background Tones:** 
  - Pure Canvas (`#FFFFFF`): Standard card surfaces, elevated modals, and crisp reading spaces.
  - Pearl Grey (`#F9FAFB`): Alternating section rhythm to prevent visual fatigue and organize content blocks cleanly.
  - Border Tone (`#E5E7EB`): Structural dividers and card contours ensuring clean edge definition.

## Typography

The type system is powered exclusively by **Manrope**, selected for its geometric legibility, balanced apertures, and solid structural integrity across mobile screens and outdoor reading conditions.

### Structural Roles
- **Headlines (Display, Headline-LG, Headline-MD):** Rendered in Bold (700) and Extra-Bold (800) with slight negative tracking to deliver high authority and instant comprehension.
- **Body Copy:** Set at regular weight (400) with generous line heights (1.5–1.75) to maintain effortless scanning for service descriptions, diagnostic explanations, and warranties.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Weighted at Semi-Bold (600) and Bold (700) with slight positive tracking to ensure high legibility on physical badges (e.g., "Garantía 12 Meses", "Cabina de Pintura"), numeric metrics ("30+ Años"), and action buttons.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system engineered for quick diagnostic scanning and prominent actionable touchpoints.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, max content container of `1280px`, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` side margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `1.25rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** 4-column grid, `1rem` gutters, and `1rem` margins. Sticky bottom bar for critical actions (Direct Call / Google Maps Navigation).

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical section spacing follows an alternating cadence: standard sections employ `space-xl` (2.5rem) to `4rem` padding, while prominent hero and contact bands expand up to `5rem` to emphasize physical shop imagery and map integration.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid approach: clean tonal containment bolstered by crisp ambient drop shadows and subtle technical outlines.

### Depth Layers
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F9FAFB` defining the background environment.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** Elevated using a 1px border (`#E5E7EB`) coupled with a soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Quick-Actions):** Elevated on hover or tap via `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)` with a subtle upward transform (`-2px`).
- **Surface Level 3 (Sticky Action Bars & Modals):** Positioned with crisp high-contrast separation: `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.15)`.

No dramatic glassmorphism or muddy drop shadows are allowed; surfaces must remain clean, direct, and structural.

## Shapes

The design system employs a consistent **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to combine mechanical precision with a friendly, welcoming neighborhood feel.

### Geometric Scale
- **Base Rounding (`rounded-md` - 0.5rem / 8px):** Form inputs, service chips, filter badges, and table elements.
- **Card & Surface Rounding (`rounded-xl` - 1rem / 16px):** Primary service cards, testimonials, technical guarantee blocks, and map containers.
- **Action Elements (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`):** CTA buttons use `rounded-xl` for large touch surfaces, instilling a comfortable, physical push-button sensation.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary CTA ("Pedir Cita Previa"):** Solid `#EA580C` background, white text, bold label, `rounded-xl`, min height of 48px (54px on mobile sticky anchors). Hover state: `#C2410C`. Focus: 3px outline in `#EA580C` with 2px offset.
- **Direct Emergency / Call Button ("Llamar Ahora"):** Solid `#DC2626` background or deep `#111827` base with an integrated vibrant phone icon, high-contrast white typography.
- **Secondary Action ("Cómo Llegar - Actur"):** Outline variant with 1.5px `#1F2937` or `#E5E7EB` stroke, `#111827` text, and location pin icon. Hover shifts to `#F9FAFB`.

### Service Cards & Diagnostic Cards
- **Base Structure:** White (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 16px radius (`rounded-xl`), framed with `1px solid #E5E7EB`.
- **Content Flow:** Icon badge in light orange tint (`#FFF7ED` with `#EA580C` stroke/glyph), followed by `headline-sm`, short diagnostic description (`body-sm`), and a quick action link ("Saber más" or "Presupuesto").

### Trust & Reputation Badges
- **Experience Seal:** Prominent container highlighting "30 Años en el Actur", utilizing `#1F2937` background, metallic or bright orange accents, and `label-sm` monospaced or tracking details.
- **Chips & Filter Pills:** Light grey fill (`#F3F4F6`), text `#374151`, transition to active `#111827` background with white text when toggled (e.g., "Mecánica Rápida", "Chapa y Pintura", "Pre-ITV", "Neumáticos").

### Forms & Quote Request Inputs
- **Fields:** Single and multiline text inputs featuring a 1px border (`#D1D5DB`), crisp white background, `rounded-md` (8px), and 44px min height. Active focus transitions border to `#EA580C` with a matching 2px outer glow (`rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** High-contrast square/circle controls with solid `#EA580C` fill upon selection.

### Sticky Mobile Utility Dock
- Fixed bottom component on mobile viewports containing two side-by-side full-width touch buttons: one for immediate phone dialing (`#DC2626`) and one for GPS navigation directly to the Actur workshop (`#111827`).
